Privacy Policy for Rentala

Effective Date: October 24, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Christian Tucay ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Rentala mobile application ("the App"). Rentala is an offline-first rental property management application designed for independent landlords and rental property managers.

1. Offline-First Architecture & On-Device Data Storage

Rentala is built as an offline-first application. All operational landlord, property, and tenant records you enter or generate within the App are stored locally on your device inside app-private storage, rather than on developer-operated cloud databases.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Property & Unit Records: Property areas, property names, physical addresses, unit identifiers, occupancy details, and unit status.
  • Tenant & Occupant Information: Tenant names, contact details (phone numbers, email addresses), dates of birth, nationality, applicant/active/former status, co-occupant names, and occupant relationships.
  • Lease & Document Records: Lease records, rental amounts, due dates, security deposits, lease terms, agreement drafts, generated lease agreement PDFs, digital signatures, and related attachments.
  • Financial & Accounting Ledgers: Payment dues, partial and full payment records, running balances, generated receipts, income and expense logs, financial adjustments, accounting ledger entries, statements, and financial dashboard summaries.
  • Reminders & Templates: Local notification schedules, reminder preferences, and custom message templates.
  • Local Security & Preferences: Local application PIN data and app configuration settings.

How On-Device Data Is Protected

  • SQLCipher Encrypted Database: Local relational records are stored in an encrypted Room/SQLite database secured with SQLCipher.
  • Encrypted Document Storage: Locally generated documents, signatures, and receipts are stored inside encrypted local storage and Android app-private directories.
  • Preferences DataStore: Application settings and preferences are stored within Android Preferences DataStore.

We do not perform automatic background cloud synchronization of your database, and we do not have access to your local database records, tenant files, or financial ledgers.

2. User Authentication & Security Modes

  • Local Device Authentication (PIN & Biometrics): You may lock the App using a local PIN and optional biometric unlock mediated through the AndroidX Biometric API. Biometric verification is handled strictly by your device's operating system and hardware keystore; Rentala never accesses, stores, or transmits raw biometric data.
  • Firebase Authentication (Optional Email & Password): When Firebase Authentication is enabled and used, your email address, password credentials, and Firebase User ID are processed by Google Firebase Authentication to verify your identity and manage account access.
  • Google Account Authorization (Optional Google Drive Backup): If you connect your Google Account for backups, authorization is handled via Google Identity / Credentials and Google Play Services Auth strictly to enable user-initiated backup operations to your personal Google Drive.

3. Optional Backups & User-Initiated Google Drive Integration

Rentala does not automatically synchronize your operational database to the cloud. You may explicitly choose to:

  • Export or import encrypted or plaintext backup files through the Android Storage Access Framework (system file picker).
  • Upload, list, download, and restore an encrypted or plaintext backup file in your personal Google Drive account.
Limited Google Drive Scope (drive.file): Rentala requests only the scoped https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission via the Google Drive REST API (using OkHttp). This allows Rentala to access only the visible Rentala Backups folder and backup files created by the App. Rentala cannot view, access, or modify any other files or folders in your Google Drive. Rentala's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Rentala does not include advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) and does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics). The App integrates the following services strictly for functionality and security:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Processes email/password authentication credentials when configured.
  • Google Firebase App Check: Verifies that requests originate from an authentic instance of the App and an untampered device.
  • Google Firebase AI: Processes prompts or inputs submitted to AI-assisted features to generate requested outputs.
  • Google Play Services Auth & Google Drive API: Facilitates optional Google account authorization and user-initiated backup transfers to your Google Drive.

You can review Google's data practices in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Android Permissions & System-Mediated Features

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used for network status checks, Firebase Authentication/App Check/AI requests, and optional user-initiated Google Drive backup operations.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display local scheduled notifications for rent due dates, lease expirations, and reminders.

No Camera or Broad Storage Permissions: Rentala does not declare camera or broad media/filesystem storage permissions. Exporting files, printing PDFs, and sharing receipts or reminders are performed exclusively through user-initiated Android system intents (Storage Access Framework file picker, Android Print Framework/WebView, FileProvider, and the Android Share Sheet).

6. Data Security

  • Encryption at Rest: Local databases are encrypted with SQLCipher, and local documents and receipts are stored in encrypted app-private storage.
  • Encryption in Transit: All external communications with Firebase and Google Drive APIs are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.

7. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

  • Local App Data: You can delete records directly inside the App at any time, or erase all local data permanently by clearing the App's storage in your Android system settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Google Drive Backups: You can delete backup files directly from the Rentala Backups folder in your Google Drive, and revoke Rentala's access at any time from your Google Account Connections page.
  • Firebase Authentication Account Deletion: If you use Firebase Authentication, you may delete your account within the App (where enabled) or request permanent deletion of your Firebase Authentication account record by emailing ctucay.terre@gmail.com with the subject line "Rentala Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.

8. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

Rentala is intended for adult users (18+) who manage rental properties. The App does not target or knowingly collect information from children under 13.
