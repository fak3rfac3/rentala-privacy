Privacy Policy for Rentala

Effective Date: October 26, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Christian Tucay ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the Rentala mobile application ("the App"). Rentala is an offline-first rental-management app for independent landlords. It does not require a Rentala account. This policy distinguishes information stored on your device from information you choose to export, share, back up, or submit to Google Play.

In brief: Rental records are stored on the device where you use Rentala. Rentala does not automatically sync them to a developer-operated server. Optional Google Drive backup and file export are user-initiated and can transfer a copy of the backup outside the device. Google Play separately processes any in-app purchase when that feature is available.

1. Information You Store in Rentala

Rentala works offline and stores the operational records you enter or create in the App in app-private storage on your device. The information depends on the features you use and may include:

  • Landlord, property, and unit details: names, addresses, property areas, unit identifiers, occupancy, and status.
  • Tenant and occupant details: names, contact information, dates of birth, nationality, status, relationships, and other details entered by the landlord.
  • Identity and agreement details: civil status, addresses, government ID types and numbers, issue details, lease terms, agreement drafts, and user-drawn signature images where supplied.
  • Rental and accounting records: rent amounts, dues, full or partial payment records, balances, receipts, income, expenses, adjustments, and reports.
  • Reminder and message information: due dates, notification preferences, and message templates.
  • App settings and security data: theme, language, preferences, and a salted PIN verifier for the optional app lock.

On-device protection

  • The Room database is encrypted with SQLCipher. Its database key is protected using the Android Keystore.
  • Managed local documents, including applicable signatures and receipts, are encrypted with AES-GCM in app-private storage.
  • The optional app-lock PIN is stored as a salted, iterated verifier rather than as the PIN itself. Biometric matching is performed by Android; Rentala does not access or store raw biometric data.
  • Some non-secret app preferences are stored locally using Android Preferences DataStore. Encryption at rest does not protect data from someone who can use an already-unlocked or compromised device.

Android automatic backup for Rentala app data is disabled. A database export, PDF, shared file, or Google Drive backup that you create is a separate copy and follows the destination and protection you choose.

2. Accounts, App Lock, and Notifications

Rentala currently uses a local device profile; email/password registration and Firebase sign-in are not part of the current app flow. A Google account is needed only if you choose to connect Google Drive. Google handles that authorization, and Rentala uses the authorized account identity to show the connected account and perform the requested backup actions. The account email is stored locally in the app's Keystore-backed encrypted vault.

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, Rentala schedules local notifications on your device for dates you configure. Depending on the reminder and your device's notification settings, a notification may show a tenant name, property, due date, or amount on the lock screen. You can change notification permissions and lock-screen visibility in Android settings or adjust reminders in the App.

3. Optional Export, Sharing, and Google Drive Backup

Rentala does not automatically upload or synchronize your rental records. You choose when to export or transfer a backup:

  • You can export or import a backup through the Android system file picker. Depending on the export option, a backup may be password-encrypted or readable without a password. Keep the password separate from the backup; Rentala cannot recover a forgotten password.
  • If you connect Drive, you can manually upload, list, download, and restore backup files in the visible My Drive → Rentala Backups folder. Backup contents are transferred to and stored by Google under your Google account. The file remains encrypted only if you exported or selected an encrypted backup.
  • You may print or share documents and receipts using Android's print, file, or share interface. The system destination or app you select receives the content you choose to send and handles it under its own terms and privacy practices.
Google Drive access: Rentala requests the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope for its user-initiated backup workflow; it does not request broad access to all Drive files. Drive access is limited by the scope and Google's authorization rules to files the App creates or that are otherwise made available to it. You can revoke Rentala's access from your Google Account connections. Google API data use is subject to Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-Party Processing and Purchases

The current app does not use advertising, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics. It does not send locally stored tenant, property, lease, or accounting records to Firebase for sign-in, AI, analytics, or crash reporting. Google services are used for the following user-requested or platform functions:

  • Google Drive and Google Identity: Used only when you connect Drive and initiate backup actions. Google receives the authorization request and the backup file and related file metadata needed to store and retrieve it.
  • Google Play Billing: If a Premium one-time purchase is available in your version of the App, Google Play handles checkout and payment details. Rentala receives the signed purchase result needed to verify and restore the entitlement, and stores purchase evidence locally in the Keystore-backed vault. Rentala does not receive your payment card or bank credentials, and does not operate a purchase-verification server.
  • Android system services: The system file picker, print service, notification service, and apps you explicitly choose to share with may process the files or notification content needed for the action you request.

Google Play and Google Drive data are also subject to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable service terms. We do not operate a cloud account system or automatic synchronization service for Rentala records.

5. Network Access and Android Permissions

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Support optional Google Drive backup and Google Play Billing/network availability checks.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Lets the App show local reminders when you allow notifications.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Lets the App reschedule configured local reminders after the device restarts.
  • VIBRATE: Supports notification or interaction feedback.

Rentala does not request camera or broad media/filesystem storage permissions. File export/import, PDF printing, and sharing are initiated through Android's system picker, print framework, or share interface.

6. Data Retention and Your Choices

  • On-device records: Records remain in Rentala until you edit or delete them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the app-private copy, but does not delete files you exported, shared with another app, or uploaded to Drive.
  • Drive backups: Backups remain in your Google Drive until you delete them from the Rentala Backups folder. Revoking authorization stops future access but does not delete existing backup files.
  • Exported and shared files: Copies saved through the file picker, print destination, or another app are controlled by that destination. Delete them there when no longer needed.
  • Purchase records: Google Play retains transaction records under its own policies. The App keeps local signed purchase evidence as needed to restore your entitlement.

7. Privacy Rights and Requests

You can review, correct, and delete records on the device where they are stored using the App's available controls. The Rentala developer does not have routine access to those local records. If you are a tenant or occupant whose information was entered by a landlord, contact that landlord or property manager to ask about access, correction, or deletion of the local record. The landlord/operator is responsible for deciding how those records are used and for providing any notices required by applicable law. You may also contact us about information that you directly sent to the developer or about this policy at the address below. Requests are handled according to applicable law; we cannot retrieve or erase data from a device or Google Drive account we do not control.

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to be informed about, access, correct, object to, or request blocking/deletion of personal information, and may raise a concern with the relevant privacy regulator. For information about rights under the Philippines Data Privacy Act, see the National Privacy Commission's data subject rights guidance.

8. Children's and Occupant Information

Rentala is designed for adult landlords and property managers; it is not directed to children. A landlord may enter information about occupants who are minors, including a name or relationship. The landlord/operator must ensure that collecting and using that information is lawful and provide any required notices or obtain any required permissions. Rentala does not independently verify a person's age or the landlord's authority to enter the information.

9. Security Limitations

We use Android app-private storage, SQLCipher for the database, Android Keystore-backed key protection, and encryption for managed documents. Network transfers to Google services use HTTPS. These safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee that data will be secure in every circumstance. Anyone who can use an unlocked device, access an exported unencrypted backup, or access your Google account may be able to see the information available to them. Use a strong device lock, protect backup passwords, and avoid sharing unredacted tenant records when requesting support.

We may update this Policy when Rentala's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Review it periodically, especially after an app update.
